8th Wall AR

Point your camera at the painted sharks on the sidewalk and one swims up to you. Tap the ground to drop your own — plus Athena and the Leaning Tower in Little Italy, a jumping shark at the Guadalupe River and a party at SAP Center. Photo Mode puts Sharkie, Sammy or Athena in your picture. Or place a soccer goal and take a shot for International Football.

Walk up to the Japanese American Internment Memorial in Japantown and the bronze relief comes to life. Open-source image tracking (MindAR), GPS-gated — works on iPhone and Android, no app needed.
